# Privacy policy — Smart Router for Claude

Last updated: October 2026

Smart Router for Claude is a browser extension that runs only on claude.ai.

## What it does with your data
- It reads the text of a prompt in the claude.ai message box at the moment you send it, in order to estimate the prompt's difficulty. This analysis happens entirely inside your browser.
- It stores your settings (mode, preferences), routing statistics (counts, difficulty scores, thumbs up/down), and which Claude models your account offers, using Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

## What it does not do
- It does not transmit your prompts, conversations, statistics, or any other data to the extension author or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
- It does not collect personal information, browsing history, or analytics.
- It does not read or modify any website other than claude.ai.

## Data retention and deletion
All data stays on your device. You can clear it at any time with the "Reset stats" and "Reset learned thresholds" buttons in the extension popup, or by removing the extension.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL and the extension's store listing will be updated.
